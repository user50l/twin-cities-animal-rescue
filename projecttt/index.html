Our Mission

Twin Cities Animal Rescue is a community nonprofit dedicated to rescuing, rehabilitating, and rehoming animals in need across the Minneapolis–Saint Paul area. We believe every animal deserves a safe, loving home and that our community is stronger when we care for the most vulnerable among us.

Our Impact

This year we have:

  • Helped more than 420 animals find permanent homes
  • Supported 95 active foster families
  • Logged over 3,200 volunteer hours
  • Partnered with 12 local clinics and community organizations

How You Can Help

You can make a difference by adopting, fostering, volunteering, or spreading the word. Visit our Services page to learn about adoption and foster opportunities, or go to our Contact page to tell us how you would like to get involved.
