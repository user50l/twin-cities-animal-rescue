Our Story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue began as a small group of neighbors who saw animals struggling in overcrowded shelters and decided to act. What started as weekend transport runs and temporary fosters has grown into a volunteer-powered nonprofit that partners with clinics, community groups, and families across the metro area.

We remain focused on direct care, responsible adoption, and building a network of people who believe animals deserve second chances.

Core Values

  • Compassion: Every animal is treated with patience and respect.
  • Transparency: We communicate openly with adopters, fosters, and partners.
  • Community: Lasting change happens when neighbors work together.
  • Responsibility: We prioritize animal welfare and thoughtful placements.

Our Partners

We are grateful for the veterinarians, pet supply businesses, local businesses, and community organizations that support our mission through medical care, donations, event space, and outreach. Their partnership makes it possible for more animals to move from crisis to comfort.
